Cinnamon Apple Bread

This Cinnamon Apple Bread has a scrumptious swirl of brown sugar and apples running through the tender loaf. A drizzled of cinnamon glaze adds sweetness and a hint of spice to the golden brown top.

Prep 15 mins | Bake 50 mins | Total 1 hr 5 mins

This easy Cinnamon Apple Bread recipe is loaded with cinnamon sugared apple pieces swirled throughout the most tender moist crumb. All the best flavors of fall in a single loaf! And here is how to make it!

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened, (1 stick)
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk

Apple Mixture:

  • 1/2 cup light brown sugar, packed
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • 1 large apple, peeled, cored, and chopped into small pieces

Topping:

  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon

Glaze:

  • 3/4 cups confectioners' sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 2 tablespoons water

Instrutions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit. Grease and flour an 8.5 x 4.5 inch loaf pan.
  2. In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder and salt. Set aside.
  3. Using an electric handheld or stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat the butter, sugar and vanilla together, on medium-high speed until smooth and pale (about 3-4 minutes). Scrape down the sides and the bottom of the bowl with a rubber spatula as needed.
  4. Beat in the eggs, one at a time, until incorporated.
  5. With the mixer on low speed, add about half of the flour mixture, alternating with half the milk. Add the remaining flour mixture and milk and mix to combine.
  6. In a mixing bowl, combine the brown sugar, ground cinnamon and apples. Set aside.
  7. To make the topping, in a small bowl combine the brown sugar and cinnamon. Set aside
  8. Pour half of the batter into the prepared loaf pan and top it with half of the apple mixture. With a knife or skewer, swirl through the batter lightly. Don’t over do it. The bread looks better when it has a beautiful thick layer of fruit and cinnamon sugar in the middle.
  9. Pour the remaining batter on top and then the remaining apple mixture. With a knife or skewer, swirl through the batter a couple of times in both directions. Sprinkle the cinnamon sugar mixture on top.
  10. Bake for about 45 to 55 minutes or until a cake tester or toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Only a few moist crumbs should attached to the cake tester or toothpick due to the soft apples in the center.
  11. Remove the bread from the oven and allow it to cool in the pan for about 15 – 20 minutes. While the bread is cooling, whisk together the glaze ingredients. Remove the bread from the pan and place it on a cooling rack. Drizzle the glaze over the bread. Let the bread cool completely before slicing.

Chai Spice Cookies

These chai cookies are an easy to make cookie! They are a brown butter chai spiced sugar cookie that are rolled twice in chai sugar. They taste just like having chai tea!

Prep 25 mins | Bake 13 mins | Chilling 40 mins | Total 1 hr 18 mins

I wanted to make a chai cookie that tastes like a chai latte, but without any tea. This cookie is chai spiced! It has 5 spices in both the cookie and chai sugar! They get rolled twice in the sugar to give it even stronger flavor. I have tips and tricks and process photos to easily help you make this recipe!

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup 2 tablespoons, unsalted butter
  • 2 2/3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 teaspoon cardamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1 1/2 cups white granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 teaspoon pure vamilla extract

Chai Sugar:

  • 1/2 cup white granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon cardamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves

Instrutions:

  1. In a large frying pan, melt the butter. Once melted, let it cook until the milk deposits separate and turn amber in color. Watch it closely while it start to separate!
  2. Pour butter into a bowl and let it cool for 40 minutes, you can speed this up by placing in the fridge and mixing every 10 minutes until cooled.
  3. In a medium bowl, sift the flour, cornstarch, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, cardamon, and cloves. Set aside.
  4. In a large mixing bowl, add the melted brown butter, sugar, eggs, and vanilla and whisk together.
  5. Add the dry ingredients and mix using a baking rubber spatula. Mix until combined. This will be a thick dough.
  6. Preheat oven to 350℉. Line two cookie sheets with parchment paper. Let the dough rest for 10 minutes to prevent spreading.
  7. In a bowl, combine sugar, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, cardamon, and cloves. Use a large cookie to scoop the dough. Roll each ball into chai sugar.
  8. Place 6 cookie dough balls per cookie sheet. Bake for 13-15 minutes until the edges are lightly golden.
  9. Let the cookies sit on the hot pan for 6 minutes before taking the cookie and rolling the top of the cookie back in the chai sugar. Place on a wire rack to cool down.

Pumpkin Cheesecake Cookies

I am extremely excited to share these Pumpkin Cheesecake Cookies with you! They’re absolutely perfect. They’re soft, chewy, spiced just right and have sweetened cream cheese running through the center of the cookie. This pumpkin cookie recipe is easy to make and needs to be added to your Fall dessert baking list!

Prep 40 mins | Bake 12 mins | Chilling 40 mins | Total 52 mins

Alright, I am in full pumpkin dessert baking mode. I get like this every year and it’s probably the happiest I am throughout the entire year. Baking makes me happy. Pumpkin desserts make me happy. It’s that simple. What makes me really happy, though, are these Pumpkin Cheesecake Cookies.

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  • 1/2 cup dark brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup granulated suagr
  • 1/3 cup pumpkin puree (blotted well with paper towels to remove moisture)
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice

Cheesecake Filling:

  • 6 ounces chilled cream cheese
  • 3 tablespoons granulated suagr
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

Pumpkin Pie Spice Sugar Coating:

  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice

Instrutions:

  1. Beat cream cheese, sugar and vanilla in a bowl until smooth using a mixer or hand-held mixer. Scoop about 1 1/2 teaspoon sized portions onto a parchment-line sheet and freeze for at least 30 minutes. You may have extra, discard.
  2. Melt butter in a medium bowl. Let cool slightly. Whisk in brown sugar and granulated sugar until incorporated. Next, whisk in blotted pumpkin (blot well with a paper towel to remove moisture), egg yolk and vanilla. Then, whisk in baking powder, baking soda, pumpkin pie spice and salt. Fold in the flour using a rubber spatula. The dough will be sticky. Cover and refrigerate for 45 minutes or freezer for at least 20 minutes.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees. In a small bowl, combine 1/4 cup granulated sugar and 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice.
  4. Using a cookie dough scoop, measure about 2 tablespoons of chilled cookie dough, flatten into a disc, place the frozen cream cheese balls in the center. Wrap the dough around the cream cheese ball, roll into a ball then roll into the pumpkin pie spice sugar. Place cookies 2 inches apart on a parchment paper lined cookie sheet and bake for 12-14 minutes. Mine baked for 13 minutes. Make sure to cover the cookie dough and place back in the refrigerator between batches of baking to ensure the cookie dough stays cool.
  5. Once cookies are baked, let cool for 5 minutes on the cookie sheet then remove to a cooling rack to cool completely. Store them in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days. Enjoy!
